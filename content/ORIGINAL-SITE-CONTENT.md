# Original site content (southslopemontessori.com)

This file records the text recovered from the existing website so nothing is lost
in the remake. The live site could not be fetched directly from the build
environment (its network policy blocks the domain), so the text below was
recovered from search-engine indexes of the site's own pages, plus the school's
public listings. Run `npm run pull-site` on a normal internet connection to
download the live pages and photos into `content/pulled/` and `images/site/`.

Provenance key: **[site]** = text indexed from southslopemontessori.com itself.
**[listing]** = public directory or review site (Park Slope Parents, Mommy
Poppins, Yelp, Nextdoor, Facebook). Anything not marked is new copy written for
the remake and should be reviewed by the founder.

## Home page [site]

> Imagine South Slope Montessori in Brooklyn, NY is a private school providing
> early childhood education and childcare.

> We strive to create a program which minimizes stress in a child's life and
> maximize balance, self-respect, and inborn aptitudes.

> Imagine South Slope Montessori is a privately owned school which offers early
> childhood education and child care. Its philosophical foundation is the work
> of Dr. Maria Montessori, carried forth by educators with a specialty study in
> the Montessori Method.

## About page [site]

> The approach at Imagine South Slope Montessori Spanish Immersion Program is
> holistic and aims to develop the whole child, nurturing the growth of the
> child cognitively, socially, emotionally, and physically. We believe
> children's early years from birth to six are the period when they harbor the
> greatest capacity to learn. At Imagine South Slope Montessori, we strive to
> provide an enriched, stimulating environment which fosters order,
> coordination, concentration, and independence where the child is an active
> explorer and can develop self-direction and a true love of learning. Through
> cooperative efforts of the staff and parents, we aim to give a child a gift
> that lasts a lifetime.

## Admission page [site]

> Children must be between the ages of 2 years old and 5 years old to be part
> of our program. All children must be vaccinated as per NYC Department of
> Health requirements.

> To begin the enrollment process, you must complete the enrollment application
> form and pay the $125 non-refundable registration fee. Once your completed
> application and fee are received, our admission office will contact you to
> setup a child interview and classroom observation.

The page also refers to open houses, applications, and interviews.

## Facts from public listings [listing]

- Founder, director and head teacher: Edith Villanueva.
- Spanish immersion Montessori, mixed-age classroom, ages 2 to 5. Described as
  the first in New York to offer Montessori entirely in Spanish.
- All teachers speak exclusively in Spanish with the children.
- Head teacher plus two assistant teachers (three adults), class size capped at ten.
  (Edith, October 2026: the cap is now twelve; the site says twelve.)
- Part-time schedules available; one family attended two days a week.
- The room is a dance studio at BAX with wood floors, big windows and full light.
- Lots of playground time on nice days (JJ Byrne Playground / Washington Park).
- A music teacher visits once a week. Classical music sets the mood.
- Activities listed: Spanish, music, yoga and meditation, science, social and
  cultural activities. Stations are rotated frequently.
- Business entity filed March 31, 2015.
- Second phone number on listings: (917) 765-8376.
- Facebook: https://www.facebook.com/montessorisouthslope/
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC7OSJEDwHC9Qro1v52xVMyQ
