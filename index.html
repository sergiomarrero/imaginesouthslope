Montessori first

Real Montessori. Small by design. All in Spanish.

Montessori classrooms are calm, orderly, and full of real work: pouring, sweeping, counting, tracing letters, caring for plants. Children choose their activity and repeat it until they've mastered it, with a teacher nearby to show the next step. With three teachers for at most twelve children, no child gets lost in the crowd. And every one of those moments happens in Spanish.

  • Three teachers, twelve children at most
  • Real materials at child height, one-on-one lessons
  • Teachers speak only Spanish with the children, all day
  • Outside at JJ Byrne Playground whenever the weather allows
About

For families who want Spanish fluency

English will come on its own. Spanish needs a place to grow.

Between 2 and 5, the language a child hears all day is the one that sticks. Everywhere else, that language is English. Here, it's Spanish, giving your child the setting to grow up truly bilingual.

Real Spanish Immersion, all day

Not a Spanish class. Songs, stories, jokes, the words for pouring and counting and making up after a squabble. Everything a two-year-old needs to say, said in Spanish by teachers who speak it with them from arrival to hasta mañana.

Speaking with confidence

Children who use Spanish all day speak it without shyness: on the phone with grandparents, at the dinner table, on the next trip. The language stays a bridge to connect with others, not a school subject.

Friends who speak it too

Your child spends the day with friends who play, sing and make up in Spanish, and you join a small community of Park Slope parents raising bilingual kids.

Traditions, not just vocabulary

Music, food, holidays and stories from across the Spanish-speaking world, so the culture behind the language comes along too.

Ready for dual-language kindergarten

New York City has dozens of dual-language public programs. Our children walk in already at home in Spanish, with their first steps toward reading and writing in it.

Whatever you speak at home, you're welcome.

Children understand their teachers within weeks and start answering in Spanish within months. With parents, we speak English or Spanish.

FAQ

Questions parents ask

Read the FAQ
We speak Spanish at home. What does Imagine add?

A whole day in Spanish, with other children, at the age when it matters most. At home your child hears Spanish from one or two people; here they hear it from three teachers and eleven friends, in songs, stories, negotiations over the pink tower, and the words for everything in the room. Most children's Spanish jumps within a few months, and it stays theirs.

Do the teachers really speak only Spanish with the children?

Yes, all day, from buenos días to hasta mañana. Updates to parents come in Spanish or English, whichever you prefer.

How big is the class?

At most twelve children, with three adults in the room: Edith and two assistant teachers.

Come see it for yourself.

Tours take about 30 minutes and children are welcome. We enroll year-round when there's space in the classroom.
