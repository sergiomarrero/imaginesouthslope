About us

Small, warm, and entirely in Spanish.

Imagine South Slope Montessori opened in 2015 as the first Montessori preschool in New York taught entirely in Spanish. Ten years on, we're still one mixed-age classroom of at most twelve children, led by our founder every single day. Most of our families speak Spanish at home; some are just beginning. All of them wanted a small, warm, real Montessori classroom where the whole day happens in Spanish.

Our philosophy

The whole child, at their own pace.

Imagine South Slope Montessori is a privately owned school which offers early childhood education and child care. Its philosophical foundation is the work of Dr. Maria Montessori, carried forth by educators with a specialty study in the Montessori Method.

Our approach is holistic and aims to develop the whole child, nurturing growth cognitively, socially, emotionally, and physically. We believe children's early years, from birth to six, are the period when they harbor the greatest capacity to learn.

We strive to provide an enriched, stimulating environment which fosters order, coordination, concentration, and independence, where the child is an active explorer and can develop self-direction and a true love of learning.

We strive to create a program which minimizes stress in a child's life and maximizes balance, self-respect, and inborn aptitudes. Through the cooperative efforts of staff and parents, we aim to give each child a gift that lasts a lifetime.

What you'll notice on a visit

Calm

Soft voices, clear routines, classical music in the background. Children settle quickly because the room is predictable.

Hands-on

Real materials, real work. Pouring water, slicing bananas, matching sounds to letters, building the pink tower.

Bilingual by living it

Every teacher speaks Spanish with the children all day. The language is never a lesson; it's the air in the room.

Small on purpose

At most twelve children and three adults. Every child is known, every parent gets a real update at pickup.

Outdoors often

On nice days we walk to JJ Byrne Playground. On wet days we move, dance and do yoga in the studio.

Parents as partners

Flexible schedules, quick replies, and honest conversations about how your child is doing.

Founder

Edith Villanueva

Founder, Director & Head Teacher

Edith opened Imagine South Slope Montessori in 2015 with one idea: the calm, hands-on Montessori classroom she trained in, lived entirely in Spanish. It was the first program of its kind in New York.

A decade later she is still in the classroom every day, alongside two assistant teachers. Parents describe her as warm, patient, endlessly creative, and just as caring with grown-ups as with children.

Our teachers

Three adults are in the room every day, all speaking Spanish with the children: Edith and two assistant teachers. A music teacher joins us once a week.

Our space

A dance studio full of light

Our classroom is on the third floor of Brooklyn Arts Exchange, a beloved neighborhood arts center on 5th Avenue: wood floors, tall windows, and light all day. Low shelves hold the Montessori materials at child height, a rug marks circle time, and JJ Byrne Playground is a short walk away.
