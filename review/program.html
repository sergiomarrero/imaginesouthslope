Our program

One classroom, ages 2 to 5, all in Spanish.

A mixed-age Montessori classroom lets the little ones learn from the big ones, and the big ones learn by leading. With at most twelve children and three teachers, every child is known.

How immersion works

From listening to thinking in Spanish

  1. 1

    Listen

    In the first weeks, routines, songs, gestures and pictures make Spanish understandable even to a brand-new two-year-old.

  2. 2

    Understand

    Within weeks children follow instructions, stories and jokes. Understanding always comes before speaking, and that is exactly right.

  3. 3

    Speak

    Single words, then phrases, then whole sentences, usually within months. We never pressure; we celebrate.

  4. 4

    Think

    By the second year, many children switch between English and Spanish without noticing. That flexibility is the gift.

What children learn

The Montessori curriculum, en español

Practical life

Pouring, buttoning, sweeping, preparing snack. Independence, concentration, and confident hands.

Sensorial

Materials that isolate size, color, weight, sound and texture, so children learn to notice and compare.

Language

Rich Spanish vocabulary, sound games, sandpaper letters and the first steps into reading and writing.

Math

Counting beads, number rods and the decimal system, held in the hands before it lives in the head.

Culture & science

Maps, animals, plants, the seasons, and the traditions of the Spanish-speaking world.

Music

Singing every day, and a visiting music teacher once a week.

Yoga & meditation

Simple poses and quiet breathing, so children learn to find calm in their own bodies.

Art

Paint, clay, collage and plenty of process over product.

Outdoor play

JJ Byrne Playground on nice days, and big movement indoors when the weather says no.

A day at Imagine

The rhythm of a day

A typical day. Times are approximate and follow the children.

  1. 9:00

    Buenos días

    Arrival, hang up your coat, greet your friends in Spanish.

  2. 9:15

    Work cycle

    Children choose Montessori materials; teachers give one-on-one lessons.

  3. 10:30

    Snack & circle

    Songs, the calendar, and a story in Spanish.

  4. 11:00

    Outside

    The playground on nice days; movement and yoga in the studio when it rains.

  5. 12:00

    Lunch together

    Packed from home, eaten together at the table, in Spanish.

  6. 12:45

    Rest & quiet work

    Younger children rest; older children keep working.

  7. 1:45

    Music, art, culture or science

    The special of the day, from our music teacher to hands-on experiments.

  8. 2:45

    Story & hasta mañana

    A last story, then pickup at 3:00 with a real update for parents.

Schedules

One calm day, 9 to 3

Our day runs Monday to Friday from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. It's short on purpose: long enough for a full Montessori work cycle, lunch, rest and time outside, and short enough that two- and three-year-olds go home happy rather than exhausted. We don't offer early drop-off or extended day. Families choose two, three or five days a week.
